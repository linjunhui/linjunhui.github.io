# Simon Learning Method

## Mindset 002｜Simon Learning Method — Explained

**In one sentence**: Break any big subject into tiny, learnable units, then attack each one with full focus and continuity — until you've conquered the whole field.

**Why it works (the math)**: A typical discipline holds roughly **50,000 pieces of content to remember**, each taking about **1–1.5 minutes** to memorize. At **40 hours/week**, that's about **5.2 months** to master a field. The method is built to make that sustained effort *efficient*, not faster — by removing wasted effort.

### The core formula

> 
> **Simon Learning = Clear Goal × Effective Method × Continuous Investment**

The "cone / funnel" image the book uses: **time is the drill that keeps boring forward, focus is its penetrating power, and a single discipline is the sharp tip.** You don't touch everything at once — you bore one narrow, deep hole.

### The 4 steps in detail

| Step | Action | Tools it borrows | Key output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 Choose a field** | Pick ONE subject; clarify its meaning | Golden Circle (Why/How/What), 9-Grid | A clear direction |
| **2 Set a goal** | Make the target concrete | SMART | A testable "done" definition |
| **3 Break it down** | Split into small, non-overlapping units | MECE (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive) | A study checklist |
| **4 Sustain ~6 months** | Study each unit with full focus, continuously | Schulte Grid, scheduling, removing phone distractions | Full mastery, unit by unit |

**Note on Step 3**: use MECE so your chunks **don't overlap** (no wasted rework) and **cover everything** (no blind spots). Find entry points via dichotomy / quadrant / process / composition methods.

### Worked example (from the book — Li Ming learning AI product design in 3 months)

1. **Choose**: AI product design. Why — qualify for new projects, boost competitiveness. What — AI basics, data analysis, UX. How — online courses, books, ask colleagues. **Prioritize: AI basics + data analysis (the 20%).**
2. **Set goal (SMART)**: Master core AI-product-design knowledge / complete 3 online courses + pass internal tests / 2 hours per day / highly relevant to current job / within 6 months.
3. **Break down (MECE)**: AI fundamentals, data-analysis methods, UX optimization, case studies — each independent, together complete.
4. **Sustain**: 2 hours daily + weekend review; Schulte Grid for focus; exchange with colleagues and output results.

### Watch out (the book's cautions)

- Goal must be **specific & measurable**; priority must be **clear — focus on the 20%**
- Breakdown must be **complete and non-overlapping**
- **Don't over-split** (too many tiny pieces = wasted effort)
- Keep **continuity**; schedule time **sensibly with rest**

### One-line memory

> 
> **One field → one SMART goal → split it with MECE → bore into it continuously for ~6 months.** Depth and continuity beat breadth.
