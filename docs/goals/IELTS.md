# IELTS

> Target: **IELTS Academic — overall Band 6.5**
> Method: **SMART goal × Simon Learning Method × Feynman Technique**

## 1. Set the goal (SMART)

| Letter | Stands for | My definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S** | Specific | Pass IELTS Academic with an overall Band 6.5 (no sub-score below 6.0) |
| **M** | Measurable | 1 mock test per week; writing 2 essays/week; 30 new words/day |
| **A** | Achievable | 2 hours/day + 4 hours on weekends, with my current workload |
| **R** | Relevant | Required for study / work application and long-term English fluency |
| **T** | Time-bound | Book and sit the exam within **6 months** |

> One-line version: *"Reach IELTS Academic overall Band 6.5 within 6 months, studying 2 hours a day."*

## 2. Break it down (MECE)

Four independent sections — no overlap, together they cover the whole exam:

| Section | What it tests | Daily focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Listening** | 4 parts, 40 questions | 1 section/day + shadowing |
| **Reading** | 3 passages, 40 questions | 1 passage/day, timed |
| **Writing** | Task 1 (report) + Task 2 (essay) | 2 essays/week, reviewed |
| **Speaking** | 3 parts, 11–14 min interview | 15 min/day speaking aloud + record |

## 3. Sustain it (~6 months)

Borrowed from the **Simon Learning Method**: pick ONE field, bore one deep hole, keep continuity.

- **Weeks 1–8 · Foundation** — build vocabulary + grammar; learn question types for all 4 sections.
- **Weeks 9–16 · Practice** — one full mock per week; drill the weakest section.
- **Weeks 17–24 · Polish** — full timed mocks, review every mistake with the **Feynman Technique** (explain it back in plain words until it's obvious).

### Weekly rhythm

| Day | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Mon–Fri | 2 hours: 1 section of the exam + 30 words |
| Sat | Full mock test (one section) + review |
| Sun | Writing correction + speaking practice + weekly review |

## 4. Review loop (Feynman)

For every mistake, don't just re-read — **teach it back**:

1. Write down what you got wrong in plain English.
2. Explain why the correct answer is right, as if to a beginner.
3. If you stumble, go back to the source and simplify again.

> **Track it**: keep a running "mistake log". Anything that keeps recurring becomes next week's focus.
