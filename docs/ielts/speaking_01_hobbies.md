# ✅ BV1AUbL6oE1G 合集 P1：谈论你的爱好 (Hobbies / Free Time) — Full Script
Hello, my name is Keith from English Speaking Success. I’m here to help you speak better English and get a higher score in the IELTS Speaking test.

In this lesson, I’m going to show you lots of cool phrases so you can talk confidently and naturally about what you do in your free time, or your leisure time. By the end of the video, you’ll be able to talk confidently about your hobbies: how often you do them, and why you like them.

Great. Now, let’s look at the IELTS Part 1 questions about hobbies and free time.

First question: What do you do in your free time?
A lot of students just say: “I like reading.” That’s too short, and it doesn’t show your vocabulary.

Instead, we can use phrases like:
I’m really into…
I’m keen on…
I enjoy…
I spend quite a lot of time …-ing.

So a better answer:
**I’m really into hiking. I spend quite a lot of time walking in the mountains at the weekend. It helps me unwind.**

“Unwind” means relax. That’s a nice word for band 7.

Second question: Do you have any hobbies?
Sample answer:
**Yeah, I’m keen on photography. I love taking photos of nature. It’s a great way to switch off from work.**
“Switch off” means stop thinking about work or study.

Third: How often do you do it?
You can use adverbs: regularly, from time to time, once in a while.
Example:
**I go hiking regularly, almost every Saturday if the weather is good.**
Or, **I paint once in a while, not every week, just when I feel inspired.**

Fourth: Why do you like this hobby?
Don’t just say “because it’s fun”.
You can talk about:
- it helps me relax / unwind / switch off
- it keeps me fit
- it gives me a chance to meet new people
- it stimulates my creativity

Sample:
**I love cycling because it keeps me fit and it gives me a break from my screen.**

Fifth: Are there any new hobbies you want to try in the future?
Sample:
**I’ve been thinking about learning to play the guitar. I haven’t tried it yet, but I’d love to give it a go.**
“Give it a go” = have a try. Very natural spoken phrase.

Sixth: Do you prefer to do hobbies alone or with friends?
Sample:
**I prefer doing things on my own sometimes, but I also enjoy activities with my friends. Hiking with others is more fun.**

Now, let’s look at common mistakes.
❌ Wrong: I like play basketball.
✅ Right: I like **playing** basketball. (after like/enjoy/into + verb-ing)

Another tip: Don’t memorise a long fixed speech. Learn these phrases, then adapt them to your real life. Examiners can spot memorised answers easily, and your score will drop.

Let’s recap the useful phrases:
1. I’m really into…
2. I’m keen on…
3. unwind = relax
4. switch off = stop thinking about work
5. from time to time / once in a while = sometimes
6. give it a go = try something new

Now let’s practise a full answer together.
Examiner: What do you like to do in your free time?
You: **I’m really into hiking. I go from time to time on weekends. It helps me unwind and get away from my computer.**

That’s natural, easy, band 7 style.
