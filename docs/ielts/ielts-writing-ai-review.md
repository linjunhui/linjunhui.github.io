# IELTS Writing — AI Review Prompt

> How I correct my essays: paste the prompt below into an AI, attach my essay,
> and get a band score + line-by-line fixes. This replaces a paid correction service.

## How to use

1. Write your essay (Task 1 or Task 2).
2. Copy the prompt below, and **replace the three inputs** at the top:
   - `TASK_TYPE` → `Task 1` or `Task 2`
   - `PROMPT` → the question you were answering
   - `ESSAY` → your own writing
3. Paste into an AI chat. Use the feedback to rewrite, then compare.
4. Log the score in the [IELTS weekly log](index.md).

---

## The prompt

````text
You are a certified IELTS Writing examiner. Assess my essay strictly against the
official IELTS band descriptors, and be honest — do not inflate the score.

=== TASK TYPE ===
TASK_TYPE

=== QUESTION ===
PROMPT

=== MY ESSAY ===
ESSAY

=== WHAT I WANT ===
Give the whole answer in English, in this exact structure:

1. **Band score** — an estimated overall band (e.g. 6.5) for this essay.

2. **Scores on the four official criteria** — for each, give a band and a one-line reason:
   - Task Achievement / Response (TR)
   - Coherence & Cohesion (CC)
   - Lexical Resource (LR)
   - Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA)

3. **What's holding the score down** — the top 3 specific weaknesses, each with the
   exact sentence from my essay as evidence.

4. **Line-by-line corrections** — a table with:
   | My sentence | Issue | Corrected version | Why |
   Keep the original wording where it's fine; only change what needs changing.
   Mark each issue as: Grammar / Vocabulary / Cohesion / Idea.

5. **Upgrade example** — rewrite my weakest paragraph at a Band 7.0+ level, and explain
   what changed.

6. **The one thing to fix next time** — a single, concrete habit to focus on in my next essay.

Rules:
- Do NOT rewrite the whole essay for me — I must rewrite it myself.
- If a sentence is already good, say so; don't invent problems.
- Quote my exact words as evidence for every criticism.
````

---

## Scoring reference（四项标准速查）

| Criterion | What it measures | To reach 7.0 you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TR** | Task Achievement / Response | Answer all parts; clear position; developed ideas |
| **CC** | Coherence & Cohesion | Logical paragraphs; natural linking; clear progression |
| **LR** | Lexical Resource | Range + precision; some less common words, well used |
| **GRA** | Grammatical Range & Accuracy | Mix of complex structures; frequent error-free sentences |

> **Note**: the overall Writing score is the **average of the four**, rounded to the nearest half band.
